The IMAGO Genre Thesaurus functions as a semantic extension and controlled vocabulary layer for the IMAGO ontology. It provides authoritative terminology for genre classification while maintaining flexibility for future expansion and refinement.

Each genre concept is assigned:

  • a persistent identifier (URI)
  • a preferred label
  • alternative labels where applicable
  • defined semantic relationships
  • external mappings when available

Special attention has been given to interoperability. Concepts are linked, where possible, to equivalent or closely related entries in the Nuovo soggettario, enabling integration with national and institutional cataloguing systems.

The guiding principles of the thesaurus include:

  • Terminological precision – Clear and unambiguous genre definitions.
  • Semantic coherence – Logical hierarchical and associative structuring.
  • Interoperability – Alignment with external vocabularies and semantic web standards.
  • Sustainability – Capacity for updates and conceptual evolution over time.

Overall, this thesaurus supports consistent genre annotation within IMAGO while fostering broader semantic connectivity across cultural heritage and bibliographic knowledge systems.
