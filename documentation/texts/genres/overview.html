The thesaurus organizes IMAGO genre concepts into a structured semantic framework consistent with the IMAGO ontology. Each concept is uniquely identified and embedded within a network of hierarchical and associative relationships.

  • Preferred Terms – Official genre labels used within the IMAGO ontology.
  • Alternative Terms (Synonyms) – Variants and equivalent expressions.
  • Broader and Narrower Terms – Hierarchical relationships that organize genres by scope and specificity.
  • Related Terms – Associative links reflecting thematic or contextual proximity.
  • Mappings to External Vocabularies – Cross-references to controlled vocabularies such as the Nuovo soggettario to promote semantic interoperability.

The thesaurus is represented using ontology modeling practices and semantic web standards to ensure both human readability and machine interpretability.
