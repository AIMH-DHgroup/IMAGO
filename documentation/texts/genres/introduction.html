This thesaurus has been designed to provide a controlled vocabulary for the IMAGO genres, as defined and annotated within the IMAGO ontology (https://imagoarchive.it/ontology). Its primary purpose is to ensure terminological consistency, semantic clarity, and interoperability in the description and indexing of genre-related concepts.

By establishing standardized preferred terms and structured semantic relationships, the thesaurus supports coherent cataloguing, data integration, and advanced information retrieval. It is conceived not only as a reference tool for human users but also as a machine-readable resource aligned with semantic web standards.

A further key objective of this thesaurus is to enable meaningful alignment and linking with external controlled vocabularies, including the Nuovo soggettario, thereby enhancing cross-system interoperability and enriching semantic connections across knowledge organization systems.
