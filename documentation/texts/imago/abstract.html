The IMAGO Ontology is a domain-specific semantic model designed to describe and represent Middle Ages and Renaissance manuscripts and printed editions, with particular reference to the resources curated within the IMAGO project. Developed using the Methontology framework and aligned with CIDOC CRM and FRBRoo/LRMoo, the ontology provides a structured conceptualization of works, expressions, manifestations, and items, as well as the actors, places, genres, and editorial features associated with them. It extends established cultural heritage standards with dedicated classes and properties for authorship, curation, publishing, manuscript description, genre classification, toponyms, iconographic apparatus, and textual elements such as incipits and explicits. The ontology is intended for researchers, digital humanists, data curators, and cultural heritage institutions seeking to model, annotate, integrate, and analyse historical visual and textual resources through Semantic Web technologies.
