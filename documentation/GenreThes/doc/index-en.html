Abstract

This is a placeholder text for the abstract. The abstract should contain a couple of sentences summarizing the ontology and its purpose.

This thesaurus has been designed to provide a controlled vocabulary for the IMAGO genres, as defined and annotated within the IMAGO ontology (https://imagoarchive.it/ontology). Its primary purpose is to ensure terminological consistency, semantic clarity, and interoperability in the description and indexing of genre-related concepts.

By establishing standardized preferred terms and structured semantic relationships, the thesaurus supports coherent cataloguing, data integration, and advanced information retrieval. It is conceived not only as a reference tool for human users but also as a machine-readable resource aligned with semantic web standards.

A further key objective of this thesaurus is to enable meaningful alignment and linking with external controlled vocabularies, including the Nuovo soggettario, thereby enhancing cross-system interoperability and enriching semantic connections across knowledge organization systems.

The thesaurus organizes IMAGO genre concepts into a structured semantic framework consistent with the IMAGO ontology. Each concept is uniquely identified and embedded within a network of hierarchical and associative relationships.

  • Preferred Terms – Official genre labels used within the IMAGO ontology.
  • Alternative Terms (Synonyms) – Variants and equivalent expressions.
  • Broader and Narrower Terms – Hierarchical relationships that organize genres by scope and specificity.
  • Related Terms – Associative links reflecting thematic or contextual proximity.
  • Mappings to External Vocabularies – Cross-references to controlled vocabularies such as the Nuovo soggettario to promote semantic interoperability.

The thesaurus is represented using ontology modeling practices and semantic web standards to ensure both human readability and machine interpretability.

The IMAGO Genre Thesaurus functions as a semantic extension and controlled vocabulary layer for the IMAGO ontology. It provides authoritative terminology for genre classification while maintaining flexibility for future expansion and refinement.

Each genre concept is assigned:

  • a persistent identifier (URI)
  • a preferred label
  • alternative labels where applicable
  • defined semantic relationships
  • external mappings when available

Special attention has been given to interoperability. Concepts are linked, where possible, to equivalent or closely related entries in the Nuovo soggettario, enabling integration with national and institutional cataloguing systems.

The guiding principles of the thesaurus include:

  • Terminological precision – Clear and unambiguous genre definitions.
  • Semantic coherence – Logical hierarchical and associative structuring.
  • Interoperability – Alignment with external vocabularies and semantic web standards.
  • Sustainability – Capacity for updates and conceptual evolution over time.

Overall, this thesaurus supports consistent genre annotation within IMAGO while fostering broader semantic connectivity across cultural heritage and bibliographic knowledge systems.
